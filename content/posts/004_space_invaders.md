Hey all! I built a Space Invaders clone in Godot 4 as game #3 of the 
[20 Games Challenge](https://20_games_challenge.gitlab.io/challenge/).
I will write up all about my experience learning my first game engine Godot (and all my)
various thoughts about the process after I finish up my last game. Until then, I kind of
just wanted to show this to a few people, haha.

All of the sprites are copies from the arcade version recreated to the best of my ability
in PyxelEdit--sound effects are from [classicgaming.cc](https://www.classicgaming.cc/classics/space-invaders/sounds).

Pls enjoy!
