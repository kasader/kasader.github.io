Server-side engineer based in Tokyo. Lover of mirth and gaiety. 

My focus is on distributed systems, multiplayer networking, and cloud infrastructure. I primarily work with Go, C, Kubernetes, and Terraform. 

I studied Computer Science and Japanese at [Swarthmore College](https://www.swarthmore.edu/), and spent time studying at [The University of Tokyo](https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/). During my time at Swarthmore, I worked as a project manager and was on the board of the [Swarthmore College Computer Society (SCCS)](https://sccs.swarthmore.edu). 

Other computing interests of mine include homelabbing, Lin*x systems (NixOS, Arch), and open-source development (computational linguistics). Also built a compiler in OCaml.

Outside of tech: I enjoy classical ballet (which I trained in for ~16 years), reading Japanese novels, and playing Action RPGs.
